With more than 36 years at the piano, Timothy Layden brings a rare blend of passion, versatility, and professionalism to every performance. His first public debut came at just eight years old, playing in front of hundreds at church — with a broken arm. Since then, he has performed across multiple states, building a reputation for warm, memorable musical experiences.

For five years, he held a Saturday residency at Carmella's Italian Restaurant in Dripping Springs, Texas, keeping guests engaged with live piano while they dined. His performance experience also includes weddings, receptions, churches, wine tastings, lounges, community events, and featured appearances such as House of Rock and Omni Hotel ballroom events in Corpus Christi.

His style ranges from easy listening and jazz to pop, classical, and request-friendly sets. Whether the goal is elegant background music or a more interactive live experience, Timothy reads the room and shapes the atmosphere with care.
